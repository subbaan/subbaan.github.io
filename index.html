Bunzi

A full-colour pixel-art platformer that runs inside the kitty terminal, starring a white bunny in a red scarf. It isn't made of text characters: every frame is real 32-bit RGBA pixels at 60 fps, with layered parallax backgrounds. Four levels, a SID-style soundtrack and a speedrun twist: collect every coin, stomp enemies to shave time off the clock, and chase a perfect clear.
